Wailtail Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Wailtail, operated by Homeextent Inc., collects, uses and discloses personal information when you visit our Canadian collector vehicle auction platform, create an account, submit a vehicle, apply as a dealer or participate in auctions.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide, including your name, email address, phone number, location, vehicle details and VIN, listing narratives, photos, documents, applications, inquiries and feedback. Dealer applications and verification may include business names, licensing details, business contact information, ownership declarations and representative identity and authority information.

Accounts use Firebase Authentication. We receive account identifiers, email-verification status and profile information associated with the sign-in method you choose, including Google sign-in where used. We retain auction activity such as bids, timestamps, watchlists, comments, review decisions and security or audit records. Browser storage supports sessions, preferences and application functionality. Where analytics is enabled, usage events help us understand page visits and feature use.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, verify eligibility and representative authority, review vehicle and dealer applications, prepare and publish listings, operate bidding and watchlists, answer inquiries, send service notifications, facilitate direct settlement, prevent abuse and maintain platform integrity. We also use relevant information to investigate errors, improve the service and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Public listings and private information

Published listings, approved photos and documents, public comments and selected auction activity are available to visitors. Review media and documents for personal information before submitting them for publication. Draft uploads and internal dealer verification records are restricted; administrators may publish approved copies as part of a listing.

Public dealer attribution can include business name, city/province, current platform verification status, documentation fee and informational tax handling. Display of dealer phone or website details requires applicable consent and publication checks. Withdrawing a listing does not automatically revoke previously published image or document URLs, downloaded copies or third-party caches.

Service providers and contact sharing

We use service providers for hosting, authentication, database and file storage, transactional email and optional notifications, including Vercel, Google Firebase and Resend. Information needed to provide those services may be processed outside Canada. Service-provider terms and privacy practices also apply to their own services.

We do not sell personal information to third-party marketers. Auction and inquiry workflows may share relevant contact information with the intended seller, buyer or platform administrator to answer an inquiry or arrange direct payment, ownership transfer and transport after a successful sale. Wailtail does not process vehicle payments or hold vehicle funds. We may disclose information where required by law or to investigate abuse and protect legal rights.

Notifications and choices

We send transactional messages related to applications, revisions, bids, inquiries and auction outcomes. If you grant browser notification permission, a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token may be stored to support notifications. You can revoke that permission in your browser or device settings. Notification availability depends on the enabled service and your preferences; granting permission is not a guarantee of delivery.

Retention, access, correction and deletion requests

We retain information needed to operate the service, preserve auction and verification integrity, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal obligations. Some transaction, bidding and audit records may need to be retained after an account is closed. Account deactivation is not the same as deletion of every associated record.

To request access, correction, account closure or deletion of your personal information, email contact@wailtail.com with the subject Privacy request and identify the account email and your request. This request channel is available whether or not you can sign in. Do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity before acting, and will explain applicable retention limitations.

Security, changes and contact

We use access controls and other safeguards appropriate to the service, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. We update this policy as the service changes and identify the revision date above. For privacy questions or requests, contact contact@wailtail.com.
