# M2.7 — Returning-dealer vehicle submissions

Status: **DEPLOYED TO PRODUCTION — November 18, 2026; owner two-vehicle acceptance and full CI passed.** This is a core Phase 1 readiness requirement before the genuine Founding Dealer Pilot. It supplements the original M2.1–M2.5 definitions; their deployed functionality remains recorded accurately. M2.6 optional initial-application photos remain independently deferred.

## Problem and intended behavior

Before M2.7, a currently verified representative encountered the first-time combined dealer/vehicle form again. The existing verification profile is bound to its originating case; duplicate identities and conflicting case associations are rejected. Repeating onboarding is not a supported second-vehicle workflow. This is a business-workflow gap, not merely a missing button.

Provide **Submit another vehicle** from My Vehicle Listings and route existing vehicle submission entry points to the same returning-dealer experience when the authenticated account is eligible. Resolve the canonical dealership from trusted account links and representative UID on the server. Display a read-only business summary; do not require re-entry of business/licence details or create a duplicate profile or verification case. Collect a fresh vehicle application, current terms acceptance and all vehicle-specific exclusive ownership/seller-of-record/no-pass-through declarations. Each vehicle retains separate curator approval, draft creation, restricted authoring, submission and administrator publication.

## Security and lifecycle contract

- Keep Bidder/Seller/Admin roles unchanged. Dealer submission authority comes from the canonical affiliation, representative UID and current verification, not role promotion or client-supplied identity.
- Design and validate a supported application-to-existing-profile association. Do not bypass the originating-case, duplicate-identity, revision, idempotency or audit protections to reuse a profile.
- Recheck canonical verification and expiry on trusted submission and mutation operations; never rely on a cached badge or business summary. Expiry/suspension during an open form or concurrent administrator action must fail safely without an unauthorized application, draft or partial association.
- Expired or suspended representatives see a clear status and administrator-review path. Block new dealer vehicle submissions and dealer authoring/submission actions that require current verification. Administrators retain their existing authorized management operations. Renewal/restoration must reuse the same profile and existing vehicle links.
- Preserve immutable per-listing attribution, CAD $0 dealer documentation fees, confirmed tax handling, private drafts/media, assigned-UID access, protected fields and individual vehicle review. Do not automatically publish, delete or reassign existing lots when verification changes. Preserve existing public historical attribution and suppression of current credentials/contacts.
- No multi-seat access, bulk imports, self-service business identity changes, payment integration, optional initial-application uploads or bidding redesign is included. No new cloud data/rules changes are authorized by this planning document.

## Implementation and review record

- `POST /api/dealer-vehicle-intake` provides authenticated own-dealership context and vehicle submission. It checks revoked/verified-email authentication, both protected account links, primary representative UID, canonical profile expiry/status and original case revision inside the write transaction. Client dealership IDs, verification pointers and onboarding identity fields are rejected. Context returns a minimal own-business summary, never licence/evidence/address data.
- Returning applications retain `intakePath`, trusted `dealerId`, `dealerVerificationCaseId` and registered UID on server-created canonical/legacy records. A private server-only `dealerVehicleIntakeAudits` record and idempotency command commit atomically. No new business profile or verification case is created. Fresh declarations/current terms remain required; token email overrides submitted email. Retries keep the submission reference across expired/suspended responses.
- Administrator vehicle approval resolves the original case from this server-owned association and rechecks case ownership, representative Auth, profile links, revision and inspected-application fingerprint. Each vehicle retains its own deterministic draft, media, approval and audit. Returning application review shows existing canonical verification and directs business verification management to the original onboarding application, preventing competing cases.
- My Vehicle Listings exposes Submit another vehicle for affiliated representatives, including bidder-role accounts with no existing lots. Standard submission entry points detect the same affiliation. A read-only summary replaces business/licence inputs; current/expired/suspended/unavailable messages and a contact path preserve vehicle entries after failure. A live status change is enforced at submission even if a form was already open.
- Existing seller draft read/write/media/submission eligibility guards remain in use. Existing public attribution/badge/contact behavior remains unchanged. No financial, publication, role, multi-seat or profile-reassignment capabilities were added. No Firebase rules change or deployment is required by the new server-only audit collection; default-deny behavior is tested. No production writes or fictional Jaguar publication were performed.
